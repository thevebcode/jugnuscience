Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy covers Jugnu Publisher, an internal tool of the Jugnu Science channel, and this website.

What the tool does

Jugnu Publisher uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publish times, and add them to playlists, on the Jugnu Science YouTube channel only. It is used only by the channel owner.

What data it uses

  • Google account authorisation for the channel owner's own channel (OAuth token), stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Basic channel information (channel name and ID) to confirm the right channel.
  • The owner's own video files and their text (title, description, tags).

The tool does not collect, store or share any data about viewers or any other users, and it does not sell any data.

YouTube and Google

By using a product that uses the YouTube API Services you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This website

This site is static (GitHub Pages). It sets no cookies and has no analytics.
